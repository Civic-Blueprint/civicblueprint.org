# Website Design Workbook

> Copy this file to your own project, rename it (e.g. `WORKBOOK-myproject.md`),
> and fill in the blanks **in order, top to bottom**. Replace each
> `> _..._` prompt with your answer. Where you can't answer confidently, leave
> it and write `OPEN:` plus the question you still need to resolve — the open
> items are your research list, not a failure.
>
> When this workbook is full, you have your brief, your UX direction, your design
> tokens, and your copy spine. Building is then mostly transcription.

---

## How to fill this out

- **Write sentences, not keywords.** "Busy founders who distrust agencies"
  beats "B2B." The sentences become copy later.
- **One idea per answer.** If you need "and," you probably have two answers.
- **Prefer the concrete.** A named real person beats a persona. A real
  competitor beats an abstraction.
- **Do not jump to visuals.** Resist choosing colors/fonts until Part 3. If you
  already have a brand, that's fine — record it as an _anchor_ in Part 3, don't
  let it drive Parts 1–2.

## Done?

When the blanks are full, you have:

- a brief (Part 1)
- a UX direction and IA (Part 2)
- a design system with real tokens (Part 3)
- a copy spine (Part 4)
- locked technical and validation decisions (Part 5)

Hand this to a builder (human or agent) and the result will be a site that
argues your actual case — because every visible choice came from a sentence you
wrote here.
